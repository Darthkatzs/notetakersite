Privacy Policy

Quiki - Smart AI Note Taking
Last Updated: July 2025

Introduction

Quiki ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

This app is designed with privacy in mind - your data stays on your device, and we only process what's necessary to provide our AI features.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Audio Recordings: When you record audio notes, these are temporarily uploaded to our secure servers for AI processing using OpenAI's Whisper technology
  • Text Content: Notes and text input you provide for AI summarization using OpenAI's GPT models
  • Device Information: Unique device identifier generated automatically for authentication purposes (no personal information required)

Information We Automatically Collect

  • Usage Data: Information about how you use the app's features for billing and limit enforcement
  • Technical Data: App performance data, error logs, and usage statistics to improve service quality
  • Authentication Tokens: Secure JWT tokens stored on your device for service access

Information We Don't Collect

  • We do NOT collect personal information like names, emails, or phone numbers (device-based authentication only)
  • We do NOT track your location or use location services
  • We do NOT share data with third parties for advertising purposes
  • We do NOT store your audio recordings permanently (automatically deleted after processing)
  • We do NOT track you across other apps or websites

How We Use Your Information

  • AI Processing: Audio recordings and text content are sent to OpenAI's secure API for transcription and summarization
  • Authentication: Device identifiers enable secure, account-free access to our services
  • Usage Tracking: We monitor usage to enforce subscription limits and calculate billing
  • Service Improvement: Anonymous usage patterns help us improve app functionality and performance
  • Subscription Management: RevenueCat processes subscription data to manage premium features
  • Language Detection: Automatic language detection to provide better AI processing results

Data Storage and Security

  • Local Storage: Your notes are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage (web) and secure device storage (mobile)
  • Secure Token Storage: Authentication tokens are stored using Expo SecureStore on mobile devices and encrypted AsyncStorage on web
  • Temporary Processing: Audio files are temporarily uploaded to our secure servers for AI processing and automatically deleted within 24 hours of processing
  • Usage Logs: Anonymous usage data is retained for 30 days for billing and analytics purposes
  • Database Security: Usage data and authentication information are stored in a secure PostgreSQL database with SSL encryption
  • No Permanent Audio Storage: Audio recordings are never permanently stored on our servers
  • No Cloud Backup: We do not automatically backup your notes to cloud services - they remain on your device

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may process your data:

OpenAI

For AI transcription and summarization services

  • Audio and text data is sent to OpenAI's API for processing
  • OpenAI's data usage policy applies to this processing
  • Data is not used to train OpenAI's models without explicit consent

RevenueCat

For subscription and payment management

  • Processes subscription status and purchase information
  • Links subscription data with your device identifier
  • Enables cross-platform subscription management

Expo/React Native

For app infrastructure and secure storage

  • Provides secure token storage and device identification
  • No personal data is shared with Expo beyond standard app analytics

Your Rights

  • Data Access: Your notes are stored locally and accessible to you at all times
  • Data Deletion: You can delete individual notes or all local data through the app
  • Account Deletion: You can request deletion of your device-based account and all associated usage data
  • Subscription Management: Manage subscriptions through your App Store/Google Play account or through RevenueCat
  • Data Portability: Export your notes from the app at any time
  • Usage Information: View your current usage limits and subscription status in the app

Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any significant changes through the app.
